Shard C requires a Magic Burst on three Cachaemic foes before each dies;
three more award Metal C. Metal C converts Haunted to ordinary Curse. Skomora
uses Setting the Stage about three minutes after engagement for roughly 30,000
damage divided among targets in range. Regurgitated Swarm resets TP and applies
Paralyze; its arrow move can knock back, Bind, and apply Bio. Dark damage is
absorbed, and the boss is notably susceptible to Stun.

## Executable equipment boundary

Fresh FindAll snapshots and the six installed GearSwap job files were checked
before choosing the offense table. The selected modes use Dolo's Naegling plus
Gleti's Knife `DualSavage`, Tackle's Naegling, Kick's Tauret, Barney's Naegling,
and Maxentius on Smalls and Achoo. Barney's reviewed physical preset is his
real three-song Blurred Harp +1 configuration; it does not assume Honor March,
Aria, Marsyas, Daurdabla, or Gjallarhorn.

Version 1.1 adds only an exact-name tank-first pull runtime. Dolo's selection
directs Tackle alone; the five damage lanes release on his first hostile action
or party claim, with a two-second nonblocking fallback. The proven support and
damage policy remains unchanged. It remains adapter- and preflight-free, and
optional expert actions are guidance rather than a permission transaction.
