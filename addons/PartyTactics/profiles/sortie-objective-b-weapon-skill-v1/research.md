# Sortie B Biune weapon-skill objective

The route targets the clustered Biune elementals on the way to Gadget B. The
requirement is at least one weapon skill on each target before it dies; the
weapon skill does not need to be the killing blow. Five credited targets
provide Shard B and the next five can provide Metal B.

Version 1 uses full-party damage from the start. At 65% HP it pauses all six,
sends one learned/ready job-appropriate weapon skill, and records the
successful action packet. If the target survives, full damage resumes; the
credit remains true even when later melee damage lands. At 30% with nobody
ready, physical damage holds until TP exists rather than risking an uncredited
kill. The first production route stops at Shard B so the next parse can tell us
whether ten extra kills for Metal B repay their time.
