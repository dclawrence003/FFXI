# Ambuscade August 2026 V1: Bozzetto Breadwinner

This is the frozen August 2026 Breadwinner tactic. Its date and boss name are
part of the ID so a later monthly V1 cannot silently replace it.

- Tackleberry faces Breadwinner into the starting-corner wall.
- Dolo, Kickpuncher, Smalls, and Achoo attack from behind.
- Barney is excluded from PartyCombat targeting so the user's camera remains
  free for Housemaker duty.
- Smalls uses Stymie/Saboteur/Silence first, then bounded Breadwinner-only
  Paralyze, Dia, and Distract.
- Each Warble is relayed to Barney's reviewed controller for the matching
  Barspell. Completed Warbles rearm one Urchin Lullaby attempt.
- Dolo and Kick automatically split to Bozzetto Urchins, then resume the shared
  Breadwinner target.
- Housemaker movement, Earthshaker, Drill Claw, and Hundred Fists produce
  visible warnings. Only Dolo produces the movement alarm sound.

Required support subjobs: Barney/WHM, Smalls/WHM, and Achoo/WHM.
