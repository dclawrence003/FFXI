Shard A requires three Abject killing blows from single-target magic; three
more award Metal A. Metal A converts unremovable Taint to ordinary Poison.
Water damage is absorbed and can amplify the next TP move. The prohibition
therefore includes Water spells and Water-aligned Distortion or Darkness.

Windower's installed weapon-skill resources were used to review the automatic
mix. Savage Blade and Exenterator are Fragmentation/Scission; Black Halo is
Fragmentation/Compression. No ordered pair among those properties produces
Distortion or Darkness. Hot Shot is Liquefaction/Transfixion and is omitted
from automation because Transfixion followed by Scission can produce
Distortion.

## Executable equipment boundary

Fresh FindAll snapshots and the six installed GearSwap job files were checked
before choosing the offense table. The selected modes use Dolo's Naegling plus
Gleti's Knife `DualSavage`, Tackle's Naegling, Kick's Tauret, Barney's Naegling,
and Maxentius on Smalls and Achoo. Barney's reviewed physical preset is his
real three-song Blurred Harp +1 configuration; it does not assume Honor March,
Aria, Marsyas, Daurdabla, or Gjallarhorn.

Version 1.1 adds only an exact-name tank-first pull runtime. Dolo's selection
directs Tackle alone; the five damage lanes release on his first hostile action
or party claim, with a two-second nonblocking fallback. The reviewed Water-safe
offense remains unchanged. It remains adapter- and preflight-free, and optional
expert actions are guidance rather than a permission transaction.
